# Black Holes - Stephen Hawking

This was an interesting little book. I never read any physics books before.
This was my first one. It is the lecture that Hawking gave for the BBC Reith
Lectures about black holes. He talks about Hawking radiation. Which,
apparently, has not been fully proven yet. Otherwise he would have gotten the
Nobel prize for it.

Furthermore, he writes about the different sizes of black holes and what effect
this has on its characteristics. 

I have enjoyed reading this book and will keep my eyes open for new physics
books.
