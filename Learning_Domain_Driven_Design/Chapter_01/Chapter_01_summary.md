# Chapter 1 — Analyzing Business Domains

## Key Ideas
- **Understand the business domain** — know which part of a client's business the software supports; the product being built may not be the business's core offering.
- **Distinguish subdomain types** — generic subdomains solve common problems, supporting subdomains help the business operate, and core subdomains are where complexity and competitive advantage can concentrate.
- **Reuse generic solutions** — an online shop for a jewelry business may be a common capability rather than what differentiates that business, so it need not be reinvented from scratch.
- **Expect domains to evolve** — a business can have multiple domains, and what counts as its core can shift as it grows or competitors imitate its earlier advantages.

## Core Message
> Understanding how a business's subdomains contribute to its goals helps engineers focus custom effort on what differentiates it and reuse common solutions elsewhere.
