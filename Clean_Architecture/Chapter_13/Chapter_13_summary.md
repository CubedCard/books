# Chapter 13 - Component Cohesion

## Key Ideas
- There are three principles:
    - REP - The granule of reuse is the granule of release
    - CCP - Gather components together that change at the same time
    - CRP - Don't force users of a component to depend on things they don't need
- These three can be taken into consideration (depending on the state of the
  project) when choosing which classes are in which component. 

## Core Message
> Don't depend on things you don't need.
